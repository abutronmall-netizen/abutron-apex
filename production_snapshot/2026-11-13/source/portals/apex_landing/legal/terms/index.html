Who provides this service

APEX is a technology workspace presented under the AbutronFX brand. The identity and registered address of the contracting legal entity must be supplied before commercial launch. Until that information and any required regulatory permissions are confirmed, account registrations are applications for DEMO-access review, not purchases or access to live-money trading.

Eligibility and acceptance

You must be at least 18, legally able to contract, provide accurate registration details, comply with applicable laws and satisfy identity, location and broker-account checks when requested. We may deny or suspend access for fraud, abuse, sanctions concerns, misrepresentation, security threats or legal requirements. Create only an account you control.

Permitted platform use

You receive a personal, limited, revocable, non-transferable licence to use authorised APEX features. Do not share credentials, interfere with execution controls, scrape restricted systems, reverse engineer proprietary software where prohibited by law, or connect an account you are not authorised to control. APEX source code, marks, interface and proprietary methods remain with their respective owners.

Trading and third parties

Market and broker feeds, quotes, execution, account balances and platform functions may be delayed, wrong or unavailable. Broker terms govern brokerage accounts and executed orders. APEX is not a broker or custodian merely because it provides interface technology. A registration does not enable live-money execution, establish a fiduciary relationship, or constitute investment advice. Any regulated service requires separate eligibility and authorisation checks.

Subscriptions, changes and termination

Prices, billing frequency, taxes, available features and renewal terms must be disclosed before any charge. No paid subscription is created by completing this DEMO registration. We may make reasonable changes with advance notice where required by law; material adverse changes may allow cancellation. We may temporarily pause features to investigate faults or protect accounts.

Outages, failures and force majeure

APEX may be interrupted by internet failures, power loss, server outages, security events, broker downtime, software errors, market volatility, queueing, slippage or stale data. Orders may fail, duplicate, delay, execute at unexpected prices or remain open when controls are unavailable. Users must monitor their broker account and know how to close positions directly with the broker.
