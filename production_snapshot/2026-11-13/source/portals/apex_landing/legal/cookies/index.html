Current scope

This registration experience uses sessionStorage to remember checkboxes while you review documents. It is not an authentication credential and is normally cleared when the browser tab closes.

What can fail

The signup API does not deliberately set authentication cookies or use advertising trackers. The web server and hosting services may process essential connection and security information.

Your responsibilities

Optional analytics, advertising identifiers and other nonessential tracking must be described and given an appropriate opt-in choice before any use. You may clear site storage in browser settings.

Records and limitations

Formal agreement acceptance occurs only when you submit all mandatory boxes on the registration form and the server records their document versions.
